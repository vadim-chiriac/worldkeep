name: State
applies_to_kind: relation
constraints:
  roles_required: [subject]
---
A one-member relation: a fact about its member, carried by `when`,
`amount`, `valence`. The home of every mutable scalar property —
populations, sizes, prices-of-the-day. Lenses may chart a subject's
states as a series.
